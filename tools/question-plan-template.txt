Subject: Chemistry

Topic: Atomic Structure

Easy
What is the smallest particle of an element? | The atom
How many protons does a hydrogen atom have? | 1

Which particle has a negative charge?
A) Proton
B) Electron
C) Neutron
Answer: B) Electron

Medium
What does the atomic number tell you? | The number of protons
An isotope has a different number of neutrons. | True

Hard

Topic: The Periodic Table

Easy
Who created the first periodic table? | Mendeleev
How many groups are in the periodic table? | 18

Medium
What do elements in the same group share? | The same number of valence electrons

Hard
Why does the atomic radius decrease across a period? | The nuclear charge increases and pulls the electrons closer


Topic: Chemical Bonding

Easy
What holds two atoms together? | A chemical bond

Medium
Which bond involves sharing electrons?
A) Ionic
B) Covalent
C) Metallic
Answer: B) Covalent

Hard
Explain why sodium chloride has a high melting point. | Its strong ionic bonds need a lot of energy to break
