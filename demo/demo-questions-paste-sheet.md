## English

### Grade 6

#### Present Simple & Daily Routines

**Easy**

```
She ____ to school every day.
A) go
B) goes
C) going
Answer: B) goes

Make it negative: I like coffee. | I don't like coffee.
```

**Medium**

```
Ask a question: He plays football on Sundays. (When?) | When does he play football?

Which sentence is correct?
A) Does she likes pizza?
B) Does she like pizza?
C) Do she like pizza?
Answer: B) Does she like pizza?
```

**Hard**

```
Put in order: usually / my brother / late / gets up / at the weekend | My brother usually gets up late at the weekend.

Correct the mistake: My father don't work on Fridays. | My father doesn't work on Fridays.
```

#### Reading: Story Elements

**Easy**

```
What do we call the person the story is about? | The main character (protagonist)

Where and when a story happens is called the ____. | Setting
```

**Medium**

```
What is the 'plot' of a story? | The series of events that happen from beginning to end

A story's problem that the character must solve is called the ____.
A) theme
B) conflict
C) setting
Answer: B) conflict
```

**Hard**

```
What is the difference between the theme and the plot? | Plot = what happens; theme = the message or big idea behind it

Give one example of a 'first person' sentence in a story. | Any sentence using I / we, e.g. 'I opened the door slowly.'
```

### Grade 7

#### Past Tenses & Storytelling

**Easy**

```
Past simple of 'write'? | wrote

While I ____ (cook), the phone rang. | was cooking
```

**Medium**

```
Which is correct?
A) I was watching TV when she called.
B) I watched TV when she was calling.
Answer: A) I was watching TV when she called.

Turn into past simple: They don't go to the museum. | They didn't go to the museum.
```

**Hard**

```
Explain when we use past continuous instead of past simple. | For an action in progress in the past, often interrupted by a shorter action

Join with 'while': I read a book. My sister played piano. | While my sister was playing the piano, I was reading a book.
```

#### Vocabulary: Word Building

**Easy**

```
Add a prefix to make the opposite: happy | unhappy

Noun form of 'decide'? | decision
```

**Medium**

```
What does the prefix 're-' mean? Give an example. | Again — e.g. rewrite, rebuild, replay

Adjective form of 'danger'? | dangerous
```

**Hard**

```
Make an adverb from 'care', then use it in a sentence. | carefully — e.g. 'She drove carefully.'

What is the difference between 'unable' and 'disable'? | Unable = not able to do something; disable = to make something stop working
```

## Science

### Grade 6

#### Cells & Living Things

**Easy**

```
What is the basic unit of all living things? | The cell

Which part controls what enters and leaves an animal cell?
A) Nucleus
B) Cell membrane
C) Cytoplasm
Answer: B) Cell membrane
```

**Medium**

```
Name two parts a plant cell has that an animal cell does not. | Cell wall and chloroplasts (also a large vacuole)

What is the job of the nucleus? | It controls the cell's activities and holds the genetic information
```

**Hard**

```
Why do plant cells need chloroplasts but animal cells do not? | Plants make their own food by photosynthesis; animals get food by eating

Put in order from smallest to largest: organ, cell, tissue, organism | Cell → tissue → organ → organism
```

#### Forces & Motion

**Easy**

```
What force pulls objects towards the Earth? | Gravity

What unit do we measure force in? | Newtons (N)
```

**Medium**

```
What force slows a book sliding across a table? | Friction

A car speeds up. What must be true about the forces on it? | The forward force is bigger than the backward forces (unbalanced forces)
```

**Hard**

```
Why does a parachute make a falling person slow down? | It increases air resistance, which balances gravity so falling speed drops

Give one example where friction is useful and one where it is a problem. | Useful: shoes gripping the ground. Problem: wearing out machine parts
```

### Grade 7

#### The Human Body Systems

**Easy**

```
Which organ pumps blood around the body? | The heart

Where does gas exchange take place in the lungs?
A) Trachea
B) Alveoli
C) Ribs
Answer: B) Alveoli
```

**Medium**

```
Name the three main parts of the digestive system in order. | Mouth → stomach → small and large intestine

What is the job of red blood cells? | They carry oxygen around the body
```

**Hard**

```
Explain why the heart has four chambers instead of two. | It keeps oxygen-rich and oxygen-poor blood separate, making circulation more efficient

What happens to your breathing rate during exercise, and why? | It increases, because muscles need more oxygen and produce more carbon dioxide
```

#### Matter & Chemical Changes

**Easy**

```
What are the three states of matter? | Solid, liquid and gas

What is the chemical symbol for water? | H₂O
```

**Medium**

```
Is melting ice a physical or a chemical change? Why? | Physical — no new substance is made and it can be reversed

Which of these is a chemical change?
A) Cutting paper
B) Burning paper
C) Folding paper
Answer: B) Burning paper
```

**Hard**

```
Give two signs that a chemical reaction has taken place. | Any two: colour change, gas bubbles, temperature change, new solid forms

Explain the particle arrangement in a gas compared to a solid. | In a gas particles are far apart and move freely; in a solid they are packed closely and only vibrate
```

## Math

### Grade 6

#### Fractions & Decimals

**Easy**

```
Write 1/2 as a decimal. | 0.5

What is 3/4 + 1/4? | 1
```

**Medium**

```
Simplify 8/12. | 2/3

Which is bigger: 0.7 or 3/5? | 0.7 (because 3/5 = 0.6)
```

**Hard**

```
Calculate 2/3 of 45. | 30

Put in order, smallest first: 0.45, 1/2, 0.4, 2/5 | 0.4 = 2/5, then 0.45, then 1/2
```

```
Write 25% as a fraction in its simplest form. | 1/4
```

**Medium**

```

What is 15% of 200? | 30
```

**Hard**

```
A jacket costs 60 and is reduced by 20%. What is the new price? | 48

Share 48 sweets between two children in the ratio 5:3. | 30 and 18
```

## Computing

### Grade 6

#### Computer Hardware & Software

**Easy**

```
Which of these is an input device?
A) Monitor
B) Keyboard
C) Printer
Answer: B) Keyboard

What does CPU stand for? | Central Processing Unit
```

**Medium**

```
What is the difference between hardware and software? | Hardware is the physical parts you can touch; software is the programs that run on it

Which memory is lost when you switch the computer off?
A) RAM
B) Hard disk
C) USB stick
Answer: A) RAM
```

**Hard**

```
Why is a hard disk called 'storage' but RAM is called 'memory'? | Storage keeps data permanently even without power; RAM holds data only while the computer is running

Name one input device, one output device and one storage device. | e.g. mouse (input), speaker (output), USB stick (storage)
```

#### Internet & Online Safety

**Easy**

```
What does WWW stand for? | World Wide Web

Should you share your password with a friend? | No — passwords must stay private
```

**Medium**

```
Give two features of a strong password. | Any two: long, mixes letters/numbers/symbols, not a real word, not personal info

What should you do if a stranger messages you online asking to meet? | Do not reply or agree — tell a trusted adult straight away
```

**Hard**

```
What is phishing and how can you spot it? | A fake message pretending to be someone you trust to steal your details; look for odd addresses, urgency and strange links

Why is it dangerous to post your school photo with your location? | It lets strangers work out where you are and when, which puts your safety at risk
```

### Grade 7

#### Networks & Data

**Easy**

```
Which device connects two networks together?
A) Monitor
B) Router
C) Scanner
Answer: B) Router

What does LAN stand for? | Local Area Network
```

**Medium**

```
What is the difference between a LAN and a WAN? | A LAN covers a small area like one school; a WAN covers a large area, like the internet

How many bits are there in one byte? | 8
```

**Hard**

```
Convert the binary number 1011 to decimal. | 11

Why is data sent across the internet split into packets? | Packets can travel by different routes, use the network efficiently, and lost ones can be resent
```

#### Algorithms & Programming Basics

**Easy**

```
What is an algorithm? | A step-by-step set of instructions to solve a problem

Which one repeats instructions?
A) A loop
B) A variable
C) A comment
Answer: A) A loop
```

**Medium**

```
What is a variable used for in a program? | To store a value that can change while the program runs

What will this print?
x = 5
x = x + 3
print(x)
Answer: 8
```

**Hard**

```
What is the difference between a 'for' loop and a 'while' loop? | A for loop repeats a known number of times; a while loop repeats as long as a condition is true

Write an algorithm in words to find the largest of three numbers. | Compare the first two, keep the bigger one, then compare that with the third and keep the bigger one
```
